Web Site Critique

Trader Joes, http://www.traderjoes.com/

My first impression of the site overall was that it felt very home grown or very down to earth. It also felt very old fashioned.

The site has good flow, but I think it is fundamentally flawed in its design. While it does have navigation buttons, the main home page is very long.

The site's structure mostly makes sense, but as previously stated it seems to drag on forever.

In my opinion, the sites graphics, layout, and media added to the overall theme of the website, but again I view it as fundamentally flawed. It didn't scream professionalism and made it feel very homeade.

The site load time was instant.

The text size and color were readable and overall laid out well. The background enhanced the feel, however it was pretty inconsistent.

It did have a navigation bar, and it was one of the highlights of the website. It made the site very navigable and easy to understand.

The website theme was very fluid and consistent throughout all the pages.

The site responded VERY poorly to window resizing. When I resized it a small amount, the navigation bar was lost and the site looked very inconsistent.

The site offered very unique aspects compared to other normal grocery stores - recipes and FULL nutrition facts. This is extremely helpful to aid in meal prep or an allergy.
