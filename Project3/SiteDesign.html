Backyard Birds: Proposed Site Color Scheme

The palette is built around teal and gray-green, pulled from the greens and blues in the pelican and stork photos. A soft teal background keeps the page calm and nature-oriented. Dark teal and near-black text and headings sit on it for strong contrast.
